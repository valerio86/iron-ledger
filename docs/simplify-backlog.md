# Simplify backlog

More graphics, less text. Add a graphic only when it removes text. Keep words on warnings and buttons, keep the coach's "why" one tap away, and never rely on colour alone.

Done: Today's repeated text, week strip, body map, readiness ring, compact Fuel line, Progress trend first, and the workout text cuts.

Done in the layout recut (Today · Week · Progress, with Setup behind the gear):

- Each coach message has a kind: a question (asked one at a time on Today), a caution about today (on the plan card), a note on the week (Week tab, with "Got it"), or a win (the finished summary).
- Week tab: targets, the days as a list, the body map of sets against range, the coach's weekly notes; paging back is the history. The 7-day muscle and joint bars sit under one fold.
- Workout: every exercise is a line; the ones in progress are open and stay open, so two or three can be on the go. One set is edited at a time, one target line replaces the stacked hints.
- Today: body strip (ring, small map, joints, note) with its sheet, one-line reason with "Why?", "Something else" for every alternative, "+ Log" for cardio, weigh-in and note.
- Progress: an overview card each for Strength, Body and Volume, with a page behind each and a page per lift.
- Setup: Plan and Settings merged into one list of rows with their current values.
- The phone's back gesture closes the top layer (guide, sheet, page, Setup).
- The ? button opens one exercise sheet: how to, your numbers, and whether to suggest it.
